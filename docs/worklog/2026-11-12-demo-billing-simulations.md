# Keep the public demo usable while sandbox verification is blocked

**Outcome:** Visitors can explore labelled invoice and transfer simulations without completing KYC.

- **Keep moving:** The user chose simulations after invoice issuance and transfers remained blocked by sandbox account verification.
- **Honest outcomes:** A simulation demonstrates the workflow only; it is never evidence that Whop approved an account, paid an invoice or settled a transfer.
- **Separate visitor state:** Each browser owns its simulated scenarios so public visitors can experiment independently.
- **Easy retries:** Reset clears the simulated history and lets a visitor explore another outcome.
- **Deferred:** Real invoice issuance and transfer settlement still require validation through a provider-supported verification path. Real identity documents are not part of this demo task.
